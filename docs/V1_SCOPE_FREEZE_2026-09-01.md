# Franklin & McGrath v1 scope freeze — 1 September 2026

## Proposition

> Franklin & McGrath is a synthetic accountancy firm and open benchmark where AI agents prove they can perform real accounting work.

This document freezes the public launch scope. Any addition must replace work of equivalent effort and must not weaken benchmark integrity, privacy, or the local-first evaluation boundary.

## Included in v1

- Franklin & McGrath public branding and launch messaging.
- A clear public homepage explaining the synthetic accountancy firm.
- The credential-free UK reference demonstration and public benchmark documentation.
- Truthful UK and US workflow representation where verified benchmark episodes exist.
- The Agent League Table: verified aggregate artifacts, jurisdiction filters, critical-failure disqualification, and separate scripted references.
- Transparent accuracy, evidence, safety, cost, and human-review treatment. A dimension without a canonical measurement remains `N/A`.
- A developer path: local demo, benchmark documentation, result format, and manually reviewed registration.
- An accountancy-firm workflow-registration path with server-side validation and a configured durable storage boundary.
- Metadata, mobile layout, keyboard-accessible native controls, error states, privacy wording, and a launch checklist.
- Documented public/private and internal-legacy naming boundaries.

## Explicitly excluded

- Executing arbitrary untrusted developer code.
- A self-service submission or production-agent platform.
- Payments, subscriptions, marketplace features, dashboards, enterprise authentication, or organisation management.
- Live accounting integrations, real client data, model training, acquisition CRM, or workflow-marketplace features.
- Hidden-evaluation publication, private lead exposure, operational analytics, and deployment of this repository.

## Launch decision rule

The benchmark remains the source of truth. A public claim is only GREEN when its implementation and verification are available locally. Production hosting and lead persistence cannot be marked GREEN until Chris supplies and verifies the required deployment and persistent-storage configuration.
